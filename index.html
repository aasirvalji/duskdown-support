to look back.

Notice when your phone day ends and begins. A private morning check-in, with room for uncertainty.

The bookends of your night

Explore your last reported evening activity and the first reported morning pickup. When iOS provides enough coverage, DuskDown shows an estimated range of phone inactivity between them.

Unknown hours stay unknown

A missing report entry is not proof of sleep. DuskDown keeps partial observations useful, marks unknown hours clearly, and leaves the duration open when the data cannot establish it.

A little history. A little perspective.

Review the last seven mornings that iOS can provide. Choose an optional local reminder to look back at your night. No nightly start or stop timer.

Private by design

No account, advertising, or third-party analytics. Your activity report stays in Apple’s protected Screen Time report view. Read the privacy policy.

DuskDown reports phone inactivity, not sleep. It requires Screen Time authorization and App & Website Activity. The morning search runs from 4 AM to noon; report freshness and history depend on iOS.
