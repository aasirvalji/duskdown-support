Privacy policy · October 18, 2026

Your night.
Your privacy.

DuskDown is developed by Aasir Valji. The app does not operate a backend, require an account, or use advertising or third-party analytics.

Screen Time information

With your permission, DuskDown requests iPhone activity from Apple’s Screen Time frameworks. Reported timestamps, hourly activity, and the app’s calculations are processed and displayed in Apple’s protected report extension. DuskDown does not transmit this report to a server or copy it into a separate database.

Local preferences and reminders

Onboarding completion and optional reminder preferences are stored locally on your device. If you enable a reminder, iOS schedules a local notification with a general invitation to open DuskDown. It contains no activity timestamps or sleep total.

History and retention

Recent-night history is requested from iOS when you open the app. Availability depends on the records iOS supplies. DuskDown does not create a permanent archive of your Screen Time report. Apple manages system Screen Time history separately.

Your choices

Revoke DuskDown’s access in Settings → Screen Time. Turn off its reminders in DuskDown or in Settings → Notifications. Removing the app removes its local preferences; it does not erase Apple’s system Screen Time records.

Apple services and voluntary feedback

Apple’s Screen Time, App Store, and TestFlight services are subject to Apple’s own privacy practices. If you deliberately share beta feedback or contact support, the information you choose to share is used to answer that request. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

This support website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and contain no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking tools. GitHub may process technical request information to operate and secure its hosting service. DuskDown does not automatically send app activity to this website. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

What the estimates mean

DuskDown reports phone inactivity. It does not measure sleep, diagnose conditions, or determine what you were doing away from your phone. Missing report hours remain unknown.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, contact Aasir Valji at aasir.valji@hotmail.com. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
