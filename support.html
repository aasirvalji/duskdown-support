A few answers about permissions, partial reports, and the quiet window.

Getting started

Enable Settings → Screen Time → App & Website Activity. Open DuskDown and connect Screen Time during setup or from Guide. No nightly timer is needed. Choose an earlier morning if today’s report is not ready.

Some hours weren’t reported

A partial report still shows available observations. Unknown hours may contain unreported activity, so the app leaves the duration open. Expand About this report to inspect hourly coverage and the update time. Refresh later or choose another morning.

The report is blank or out of date

Check Screen Time authorization and App & Website Activity. iOS controls report freshness, so a refresh cannot force new records. If access was revoked, reconnect from Guide. Screen Time data requires a physical iPhone.

Why is the evening timestamp a range?

iOS provides hourly observations rather than an exact final-use event. DuskDown shows the reported activity hour and, when supported, a duration range. A quiet window can include reading, relaxing, or other time away from the phone; it is not a sleep measurement.

Morning and history limits

The app looks from 8 PM through noon, with the first reported morning pickup after 4 AM. This may not suit shift work. History requests the last seven mornings, subject to the records iOS provides.

Reminders and access

Choose a morning reminder from Guide. It is a local notification with no activity data. Notification permissions can be changed in Settings → Notifications. Screen Time access can be revoked in Settings → Screen Time.
